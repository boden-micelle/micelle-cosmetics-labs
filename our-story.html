The Origin of Our Name

Why Micelle?

In colloidal chemistry, a micelle is an elegant, self-assembling spherical structure. Its hydrophilic exterior harmonizes seamlessly with aqueous environments, while its hydrophobic core protects, encapsulates, and delivers sensitive active compounds intact into biological membranes.

That physical principle mirrors our role as a CDMO partner: we create the protective, stable structural vehicle that shields your brand's active vision, guiding it through the pressures of stability testing and industrial compounding to reach consumers intact.
