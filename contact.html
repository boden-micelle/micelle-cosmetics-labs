Technical Scoping FAQ

Common questions regarding CDMO collaboration.

How does the mutual NDA process work?

Prior to sharing proprietary active ratios or sensitive brand IP, our team executes a mutual non-disclosure agreement (MNDA) to guarantee bilateral confidentiality.

What is your typical formulation turnaround time?

Bench prototype iterations are generally completed within 3 to 6 weeks, followed by accelerated thermal stability challenge protocols.

Can you assist with tech-transfer from an existing CMO?

Yes. Our analytical chemistry team performs batch forensics and mixing diligence to replicate and optimize existing formulas seamlessly.

Do you supply testing and regulatory release documentation?

Every pilot and commercial batch includes full Certificates of Analysis (COA), micro challenge documentation, and packaging compatibility data.
